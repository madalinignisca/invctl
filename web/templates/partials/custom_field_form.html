Define a custom field

This cannot be undone selectively.

A custom field's values are recorded permanently in the audit trail, cannot be removed, and must not hold personal data. Redaction of a custom value is all-or-nothing across every custom field on every entity, so there is no way to hide one field's values later without hiding every field's. Never create a field such as "Owner email", "Contact" or "Requested by".
