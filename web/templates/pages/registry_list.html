Above the prefixes

Allocations

Where the address space came from. A prefix says a network exists; an allocation says whether it is yours — a registry delegation you keep, a slice of a provider's space that leaves with the contract, or RFC1918 that was never anybody's to give. Three different answers to "can we renumber out of this".
