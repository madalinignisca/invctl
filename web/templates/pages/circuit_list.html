Contracted connectivity

Circuits

The estate already records the port a handoff lands on. A circuit is the other half: who sells it, what was committed, what it costs a month and when the contract ends. Nothing stops working on that day — somebody either renegotiates or is auto-renewed at a rate nobody checked, and the second is why these join the expiry report.
