Outage simulation

If {{.Bundle.Name}} is cut

Every LIVE cable pulled through this duct/tray/trunk is severed at once — a backhoe does not pick one strand out of a bundle. A retired cable in this bundle is history, not something still there to be cut, so it is left out. Nothing is taken out of the estate, so anything still reachable another way is correctly reported as unaffected.
