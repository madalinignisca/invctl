Layer 2, stretched

Overlays

A VLAN is a broadcast domain on one fabric. An overlay carries one across a fabric — VXLAN over an IP underlay, VPLS over MPLS. It is the case where two ports share an L2 domain with no cable, no containment and no VLAN connecting them, so without a record here that adjacency is invisible and a trace stops at the underlay.
