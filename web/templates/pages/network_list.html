Layer 1-3

Networks

Declared forwarder groups, uplinks and anchors -- the topology model M3's reachability engine reads. A lone switch is a group of one; an MC-LAG pair or an active/passive firewall pair is a group of two. An asset with no attachment anywhere in its containment ancestry is unmodelled, never counted against anything.

Coverage
