Layer 1-3

Topology

Declared forwarder groups, uplinks and anchors -- the topology model M3's reachability engine reads. A lone switch is a group of one; an MC-LAG pair or an active/passive firewall pair is a group of two. An asset with no attachment anywhere in its containment ancestry is unmodelled, never counted against anything.

Coverage

Of {{.Coverage.TotalAttachable}} assets that can carry an attachment at all (racks, PDUs and switches never can). Modelled means an active attachment on that plane exists somewhere in the asset's own containment ancestry. Unmodelled is absence of topology data, never evidence of disconnection.
