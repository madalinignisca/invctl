Change log

Every mutation of declared state writes one of these rows in the same transaction that made the change, so an entry cannot survive a rollback and a change cannot escape without one. Diffs are field-level; a create carries a full snapshot. Nothing is ever updated or deleted here — a wrong entry is corrected by writing another one.

Observed transitions are deliberately not in this table. A thirty-second health poll is thousands of rows a day per asset, and the configuration change that caused an incident would become unfindable underneath them. They live in their own ledger and are folded back in on each entity's timeline, where the two together are what answer the question.
