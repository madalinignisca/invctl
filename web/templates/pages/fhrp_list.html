Redundancy groups

VRRP, HSRP and CARP all do one thing: several routers share a virtual address, and if the one answering for it dies another takes over. The number worth reading here is how many routers are actually in the group — one is a single point of failure wearing the costume of a redundant one, and it looks identical everywhere else.
